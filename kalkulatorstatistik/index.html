Keterangan Simbol

Singkatan variabel dalam statistik RS

A : Jumlah Tempat Tidur tersedia
t : Jumlah hari dalam periode (30 / 365 hr)
HP : Jumlah Hari Perawatan
LD : Jumlah Lama Dirawat
D : Pasien Keluar (Hidup + Mati)

1. BOR (Bed Occupancy Rate)

Ideal: 60% – 85%
BOR = [ HP / (A × t) ] × 100%
Cari HP: HP = (BOR × A × t) / 100
Cari A: A = (HP × 100) / (BOR × t)
Cari t: t = (HP × 100) / (BOR × A)

2. AvLOS (Average Length of Stay)

Ideal: 6 – 9 hari
AvLOS = LD / D
Cari LD: LD = AvLOS × D
Cari D: D = LD / AvLOS

3. TOI (Turn Over Interval)

Ideal: 1 – 3 hari
TOI = [ (A × t) − HP ] / D
Cari A: A = [ (TOI × D) + HP ] / t
Cari HP: HP = (A × t) − (TOI × D)
Cari D: D = [ (A × t) − HP ] / TOI
Cari t: t = [ (TOI × D) + HP ] / A

4. BTO (Bed Turn Over)

Ideal: 40 – 50 kali/thn
BTO = D / A
Cari D: D = BTO × A
Cari A: A = D / BTO

5. NDR & 6. GDR (Angka Kematian)

NDR <25‰ | GDR <45‰
NDR = (Mati ≥ 48 jam / D) × 1.000‰
GDR = (Total Mati / D) × 1.000‰
Cari Mati ≥48j: M≥48 = (NDR × D) / 1.000
Cari Total Mati: M_tot = (GDR × D) / 1.000
Cari Mati <48j: M<48 = M_tot − M≥48
Cari D (NDR): D = (M≥48 × 1.000) / NDR

⚡ Rumus Pintas Antar-Indikator

Tanpa Data A, HP, D
BTO dari LOS & TOI: BTO = t / (AvLOS + TOI)
BOR dari LOS & TOI: BOR = [AvLOS / (AvLOS + TOI)] × 100
BOR dari BTO & LOS: BOR = [(BTO × AvLOS) / t] × 100
LOS dari BOR & TOI: AvLOS = (BOR × TOI) / (100 − BOR)
TOI dari BOR & LOS: TOI = [AvLOS × (100 − BOR)] / BOR
LOS dari BOR & BTO: AvLOS = (BOR × t) / (BTO × 100)
TOI dari BOR & BTO: TOI = [(100 − BOR) × t] / (BTO × 100)
